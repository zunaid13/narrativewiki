\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We probed a common design, decomposing a narrative into dated claims filtered at the
reader's cutoff, and found that in our builds it trades context for accumulation: its pages
grow with the story and keep what they said, while its failures arise on evidence the
filter permits, through lost attribution and modality, identities shaped by later volumes
and statements placed out of context. Prefix-bounded prompting
disclosed no assessed later fact, and a renamed-text control exposed failures the familiar
text concealed. Our gold facts, labels and renaming pipeline make both sides testable on
longer works.
