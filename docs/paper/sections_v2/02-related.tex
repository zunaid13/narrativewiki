\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Spoilers, characters and temporal bounds.}
Spoiler detection typically labels review sentences without conditioning on a reader's
position \citep{wan2019spoiler}. Closest in aim, \citet{jones2018spoilers} serve fan-wiki
revisions archived before an episode aired; we generate cutoff-specific pages from the
source text and evaluate their content. E-readers also offer position-bounded answers
\citep{amazon2025kindle}, although we are unaware of a published evaluation.
CHIRON builds validated character sheets \citep{gurung2024chiron}, BookWorm pairs books with
character descriptions \citep{papoudakis2024bookworm}, and EvolvTrip builds temporal graphs
of characters' mental states \citep{yang2025evolvtrip}. TimeChara measures point-in-time
hallucination in role-playing models \citep{ahn2024timechara}; Living the Novel gates
conversation to the narrative present \citep{huang2025living}, using the
filter-before-generate principle we adopt; and NS-ST-GraphRAG answers from temporally valid
subgraphs \citep{lin2026nsst}. Temporal knowledge graphs attach validity intervals to facts
\citep{dasgupta2018hyte}; ours are indexed by reading volume rather than story time. These
works bound answers or dialogue in time; none evaluates a complete linked reference artifact
at every cutoff (Table~\ref{tab:prior}). Identity errors also connect to literary
coreference: LitBank annotates identities using the whole text, including revelations
unavailable at an earlier mention \citep{bamman2020litbank}.

\paragraph{Grounded article and wiki generation.}
STORM researches and outlines Wikipedia-like articles \citep{shao2024storm}; GraphRAG
summarizes a corpus entity graph \citep{edge2024graphrag}; LightRAG, adapted here as a
baseline, combines graph structure with lightweight retrieval \citep{guo2024lightrag}; and
SearchWiki builds linked wikis for agent navigation \citep{singh2026searchwiki}. Book-length
benchmarks evaluate question answering and summarization over whole novels
\citep{wang2024novelqa,chang2024booookscore}. Our reference additionally depends on the
reader's position within the corpus.

\paragraph{Factuality, attribution and contamination.}
We evaluate atomic support following FActScore \citep{min2023factscore}, attribution to
identified sources \citep{rashkin2021ais} and citation quality following ALCE
\citep{gao2023alce}, extending evaluation to assertions across the artifact and their page
context. Automatic raters of book-length text can disagree with knowledgeable readers
\citep{kim2024fables}. On STORYSUMM, MiniCheck's Flan-T5 checker \citep{tang2024minicheck}
scored below chance in balanced accuracy, and individual annotation protocols missed errors \citep{subbiah2024storysumm}; we
therefore use evidence-reading annotators rather than entailment classifiers. Published
novels may also be known to the generator \citep{chang2023speak,cheng2025contamination}; a
closed-book diagnostic measures its recall without source text. Renaming entities is an
established control \citep{gupta2025whodunit,zhang2024infinitebench}, and rewriting can
damage the narrative it protects \citep{sun2025emperor}; we add aligned paraphrase and audit
the treatment.
