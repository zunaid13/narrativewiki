\section{Qualitative Example: One Page at Two Cutoffs}
\label{app:qualitative}

Table~\ref{tab:qual-diff} compares Gilbert Blythe's page in the reported \sys site at
cutoffs~2 and~4; volume~4 first establishes his marriage to Anne. The reader picks a cutoff
on the home page before seeing any content, and each cutoff's pages use only claims whose
evidence volume is at most that cutoff (Figure~\ref{fig:overview}a shows the stored
intervals behind the relationship block). The pages themselves were not annotated;
precision labels in Section~\ref{sec:results} assess sampled atoms of the underlying pages.
The upper block of the table shows the intended behaviour of intervals; the lower block
shows four defects of the reported build.
