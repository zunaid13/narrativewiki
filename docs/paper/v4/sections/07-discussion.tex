\section{Discussion}
\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{One account of both sides of the trade-off.}
The pipeline replaces passages with claims (Table~\ref{tab:glance}). As claims accumulate,
pages can grow with the story and keep facts; budgeted rewrites in these comparisons kept
fewer (Proposition~1, Appendix~\ref{app:formal}). Volume filtering checks the date of the
evidence, while the representation weakly preserves speaker, modality, namesake identity and
scene context (Propositions~2--3). The observed failures include a prediction rendered as a
marriage, identities shaped by later volumes and true statements misplaced on a page. B4 kept a hedge that rendering dropped, but lost more facts between cutoffs on
the same graph. Extraction and publication both warrant scrutiny; stage ablations could
separate them.

\paragraph{Where accumulation may matter.}
Accumulation may pay where rewriting each page is costly: series longer than one context
window, and characters whose record outgrows a page budget. Untested repairs that keep it:
carry speaker and modality in the claim schema, validate entity groupings against the
prefix, and check each assertion in its page context (Appendix~\ref{app:recommendations}).
