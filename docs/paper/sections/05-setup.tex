\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Corpus and gold facts.}
We evaluate the first five novels in publication order of L.~M.~Montgomery's Anne series,
from \emph{Anne of Green Gables} to \emph{Rainbow Valley}, using Project Gutenberg editions:
\res{T1-anne-words} words, \res{T1-anne-paras} paragraphs and \res{T1-anne-chapters} chapters.
This series also informed system development; it is not held out (Appendix~\ref{app:corpus}).
The gold inventory contains \res{T1-anne-gold-t5} facts about \res{T1-anne-cast} characters,
each with a reveal volume and an evidence paragraph. It was drafted from the source text
without access to system outputs; eligible counts at each cutoff appear in
Table~\ref{tab:corpus}; dataset composition and examples are in Appendix~\ref{app:corpus}.
\ifnwhuman For 85 of the \res{T1-anne-gold-t5} facts the cited paragraph suffices alone;
the other 26 (23 established in part, three not) need text beyond that paragraph and
are retained unchanged (Appendix~\ref{app:annotation}).\fi{}
Recall measures coverage of this selected inventory rather than all facts in the novels.

\paragraph{Systems.}
All systems use \res{GEN-model}. Comparison systems share a character-page template,
output budget and citation format. A cast character receives a page at $t$ if an eligible
gold fact concerns them or their full name occurs in $\Dt$
(\res{PAGES-anne-cast} pages at $t=1..5$; Figure~\ref{fig:systems}).
\textbf{B1} supplies the complete prefix $\Dt$ to the generator.
\textbf{B2} combines BM25 and dense retrieval from a prefix-only index.
\textbf{B3} uses prefix-built LightRAG \citep{guo2024lightrag} as the shared generator's retriever.
\textbf{B4} retrieves from the full five-volume \sys graph, filtering facts at retrieval time;
it controls for the graph while changing page generation and publication scope.
\textbf{B5}, \sys, discovers its own entities and also publishes other character,
relationship, codex and timeline pages. We evaluate its full artifact and separately examine
the cast character pages shared with the baselines.
\textbf{X1} receives only the title and character name (closed book).
\textbf{X2} receives all five volumes with an instruction to reveal nothing after $t$.
These two diagnostics are excluded from best-system comparisons. Baseline settings were
fixed on a separate development novel (Appendix~\ref{app:baselines}).

\paragraph{Paired text conditions.}
Every system is built twice, on the original text and on a treated copy
(Appendix~\ref{app:contamination}). A reviewed map of \res{DECON-map} entries renames
characters, families and local places; the generator paraphrases each renamed
paragraph, preserving facts, order and speakers. Paragraph identifiers are kept and the
same map transforms the gold facts, so reveal volumes, citations, rules, code and
prompts carry over unchanged.

\paragraph{Metrics.}
We report four separate outcomes (Figure~\ref{fig:protocol}; Appendix~\ref{app:annotation}).
\emph{Recall} (M1) is the fraction of eligible gold facts stated of the correct character
anywhere in $\Wt$.
\emph{Precision} (M3) measures full support in $\Dt$ for 40 uniformly sampled atomic
assertions per system and cutoff, drawn from an inventory of all delivered surfaces
\citep{min2023factscore}. Assertions are judged in their page context: a true scene line
on a relationship page is supported there only if both characters participate in a shared
episode of that scene (the \emph{scene rule}). We also report statement-only judgments and
the \emph{line rule}, which requires participation in the episode described by the sampled line.
\emph{Citation sufficiency} (M8) is the fraction of sampled assertions fully established
by their cited paragraphs alone; uncited assertions count as insufficient.
\emph{Disclosure} (M4) searches every page for every assessed later fact using three
lexical routes. Retrieved candidates are labeled \emph{stated}, \emph{implied},
\emph{uncertain} or absent; stated and implied count as disclosure. There are 220
fact--cutoff assessments per system at $t=1..4$; M4 is undefined at $t=5$.
We test candidate-search recall with seeded disclosures and add two checks independent
of that search: later-only support among sampled assertions, and names in $\Wt$ that
occur in the full entity index but not in $\Dt$.

\paragraph{Annotation and review.}
Claude Opus~5.5 read evidence under written rules and finalized all reported AI labels,
including gold, recall, disclosure, precision and citation sufficiency.
Claude harmonized pair
verdicts; agreement was \res{ANN-pair-agree} (Cohen's $\kappa=\res{ANN-pair-kappa}$).
Reported values incorporate consistency audits\ifnwhuman{} and the review-triggered recall
recheck; sampled precision and citation labels were left unchanged so that one judgment
standard applies to every item\fi{}.
\ifnwhuman Two human reviewers then independently relabeled \res{HREV-total} original-text
items without seeing the AI answers ($\kappa$ from \res{HREV-P-kappa-2dp} on precision to
\res{HREV-R-kappa-2dp} on recall; Table~\ref{tab:human-agreement}).\fi{}
Appendix~\ref{app:annotation} details the review scope and corrections.

\paragraph{Statistics.}
We report unweighted cutoff means and sample standard deviations; medians and ranges appear
in Table~\ref{tab:stats}. Paired differences use shared entities and cutoffs and are computed
before rounding. Each system was generated once. The reported spread describes variation
across cutoffs, not repeated runs, and cutoff-level samples of 40 assertions support only
limited comparisons.
