\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We introduced cutoff-bounded reference generation, an artifact-level evaluation protocol
and a pilot dataset, with \sys as a reference implementation. In this five-book pilot,
\sys recovered fewer gold facts and had lower citation sufficiency than long-context
prompting. Withholding later text avoided stated assessed disclosures on the original;
\sys nevertheless implied one.\ifnwdecon{} Renaming reduced closed-book recall,
while \sys still stated a later marriage from a prediction.\fi{}
The protocol makes these tradeoffs and failure channels visible; stronger comparisons
require independent annotation, held-out works and repeated generations.
