\subsection{A held-out series}
\label{sec:results-heldout}
We repeated the comparison on a second public-domain series that no stage of development had
seen: the first five books of L.~Frank Baum's Oz series (\res{T1-oz-gold-t5} gold facts), with
code, prompts and settings frozen before its first model call. \sys conveys
\res{M1-oz-avg-B5} of the eligible gold against \res{M1-oz-avg-B1} for long-context prompting and
\res{M1-oz-avg-X1} for the closed-book generator; sampled precision is \res{M3-oz-avg-B5} against
\res{M3-oz-avg-B1}. The input-cut systems disclose \res{M4-oz-total-cut} assessed later facts and
the instructed full-text diagnostic \res{M4-oz-total-X2}; the lexical screen finds
\res{SCREEN-oz-B5} names on \sys's pages that a reader at the cutoff has not met.
